4. The weekly brief

  • We send the brief only to people who have signed up and ticked the consent box.
  • Sign-ups are handled by our newsletter service, Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France). After you sign up, Brevo emails you a link to confirm your address. You are not added to the list until you click it.
  • Brevo stores your email address and consent record for us, sends the brief, and records whether emails are delivered, opened and clicked so we can keep the list healthy.
  • Every email names Ally & Associates as the sender and carries an unsubscribe link. You can also reply “unsubscribe” or email us. Opting out is free and takes effect immediately.
  • Unsubscribing stops all our marketing emails at once.
  • After you unsubscribe, we keep your email address on a suppression list only so that we do not email you again.
  • We never share subscriber lists with third parties for their own marketing.

The weekly brief is general analysis of public information. It is not investment advice.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal data only when we need to, and never sell it.

  • Service providers that process data on our behalf: our email provider, our newsletter service (Brevo), and our file storage and accounting tools. They act on our instructions under written contracts that require confidentiality and security.
  • Associate partners, the development finance and asset management professionals who work with us on an engagement, under confidentiality terms, and only when you have engaged us.
  • Capital providers, partners and counterparties, only when you ask us to make an introduction and agree to what we share.
  • Professional advisers such as our lawyers, accountants and auditors, under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Authorities such as the Kenya Revenue Authority, courts or regulators, where the law requires it.

7. Transfers outside Kenya

Some of our service providers store data outside Kenya, mainly in the United States and the European Union. For example, newsletter subscriber details are held by Brevo in the European Union, and the website is hosted by GitHub.

Under sections 48 and 49 of the Act, we transfer personal data outside Kenya only where there are appropriate safeguards, such as contracts with data protection terms, or where the transfer is necessary for our contract with you, or with your consent. We do not transfer sensitive personal data outside Kenya without your consent.

9. How we protect it

  • Access is limited to the people who need it for the work, and they are bound by confidentiality.
  • Our accounts use strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication.
  • The website runs only over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • We collect the minimum data needed, and delete it on schedule.
  • Our service providers must keep data secure under written contracts.

If a breach puts your data at real risk, we will notify the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell you in writing as soon as practical, with the steps you can take to protect yourself.

11. Other things you should know

  • Do you have to give us data? No law requires you to. It is voluntary. Without an email address we cannot send the brief, and without contact and project details we cannot reply to an enquiry or carry out an engagement.
  • Children. Our services are for businesses and professionals. They are not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect children’s data. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.
  • If someone else gave us your data, for example a client naming you as a project contact, we will tell you within 14 days where practical, unless you already know or an exemption applies.
