        06 — Who we are

        Partner-led advisory, with specialist depth.

        Ally & Associates is a Nairobi-based advisory partnership for investors, multinationals, project owners and development partners committing capital across the COMESA and ECOWAS regions. We combine on-the-ground knowledge with a macroeconomic and geopolitical view of the continent, from market entry and country risk to project finance readiness and transaction support.

        Our partners are senior professionals in political economy, political risk, development finance and asset management. Together we bring lender, fund and institutional-investor perspectives to every mandate, so you see your opportunity the way capital providers will.

        Follow us on LinkedIn for analysis of policy and investment trends across Africa.
