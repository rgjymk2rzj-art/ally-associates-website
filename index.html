    06 — Where we work

    Rooted in Nairobi. Working across the corridor.

    The COMESA region

    Our home region. Regional entry strategy and reform-risk analysis across member states.

    • Capital movementForeign direct investment into the region roughly doubled to about US$65 billion in 2024, led by construction, renewable energy and power.
    • TradeThe COMESA–EAC–SADC Tripartite Free Trade Area, in force since 2024, widens the market for manufacturers and agri-processors across eastern and southern Africa.
    • Investment rulesA revised COMESA Common Investment Area agreement, validated in 2026, sets shared rules on investor protection and capital movement as it moves toward ratification.

    The ECOWAS region

    Mining and energy advisory across member states.

    • Financial sectorNigeria’s 2025 exit from the FATF grey list and a bank recapitalisation that raised about ₦4.6 trillion in new capital are reopening its financial sector to foreign investors.
    • EnergyNew oil and gas output in Senegal and momentum behind the Atlantic gas pipeline are drawing capital into upstream energy and gas-to-power.
    • Political riskWith Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger outside ECOWAS since 2025, reading political and mining-code risk is now part of every mining decision in the Sahel.

    Market signals as of November 2026.

    Each mandate is partner-specific, but the view behind it is regional. We follow the trade corridors and read the signals that politics, infrastructure and AfCFTA trade rules send, alongside what our investor community tells us capital needs to see.

    We don’t only follow where capital goes. By reading those signals ahead of volatility, we help shape the decisions about where it should go next.
